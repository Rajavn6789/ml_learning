# Machine Learning Use Cases

Machine learning turns data into predictions — _"What will happen next?"_,
_"What should we recommend?"_, _"Is this normal or suspicious?"_ It's the engine
behind most modern intelligent systems: recommendations, fraud detection,
medical imaging, self-driving cars, search ranking, cybersecurity, and more.

Below are the **three categories of ML** with the **three most important
real-world use cases** for each.

## 1. Supervised learning

Learns from **labeled** examples (input → known answer) to predict outcomes on
new data. The workhorse of industry ML.

| #   | Use case                                | What it does                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Fraud detection** (banking/finance)   | Flags credit-card fraud, fake transactions, and unusual logins — e.g. one card used in two countries within minutes. Used by Visa and major banks. |
| 2   | **Medical imaging & diagnosis**         | Detects tumors in X-rays/MRIs and predicts disease risk (diabetes, heart disease) — speeds up and supports radiology.                              |
| 3   | **Spam & phishing / content filtering** | Classifies emails and messages as malware, phishing, or harmful content by learning from past attacks.                                             |

## 2. Unsupervised learning

Finds structure in **unlabeled** data — grouping, patterns, and anomalies — with
no predefined answers.

| #   | Use case                                         | What it does                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Customer segmentation** (e-commerce/marketing) | Clusters shoppers by behavior for targeted offers, pricing, and inventory forecasting.                                                            |
| 2   | **Anomaly detection** (cybersecurity/industry)   | Spots network intrusions, suspicious logins, and impending machine failures (predictive maintenance) as deviations from normal patterns.          |
| 3   | **Recommendation systems**                       | Surfaces "you might also like" suggestions on Netflix, YouTube, Amazon, and Spotify by discovering patterns in clicks, watch time, and purchases. |

## 3. Reinforcement learning

Learns by **trial and error** — an agent takes actions, receives rewards, and
optimizes a long-term goal.

| #   | Use case                                  | What it does                                                                                                                    |
| --- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Self-driving & driver assistance**      | Learns lane keeping, collision avoidance, and adaptive cruise control through feedback (Tesla and autonomous-driving research). |
| 2   | **Route & logistics optimization**        | Improves delivery paths, fleet management, and demand forecasting for ride-hailing and food delivery (Uber and similar).        |
| 3   | **Dynamic pricing & resource allocation** | Adjusts prices and inventory in real time to maximize revenue while reducing stock waste.                                       |

## Quick reference: which category fits?

| If you have…                   | …and want to                | Use           |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------- |
| Labeled examples               | Predict a label/value       | Supervised    |
| Unlabeled data                 | Find groups or oddities     | Unsupervised  |
| An environment + reward signal | Learn a sequence of actions | Reinforcement |
